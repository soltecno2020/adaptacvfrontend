## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory intimacy, precision, and restrained theater of multi-Michelin-starred gastronomy. It is tailored for an executive chef whose portfolio demands the gravity of an art monograph and the functional polish of a luxury atelier.

The design movement synthesizes **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with **Subtle Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- Deep, atmospheric warmth replaces cold corporate black, evoking charcoal embers, charred cedar, and aged cast iron.
- Generous, deliberate negative space honors culinary photography as curated gallery pieces rather than mere product shots.
- Gold accents act with surgical restraint—functioning as hairline highlights, delicate status markers, and tactile foils rather than garish fills.
- Tactile surfaces pair frosted matte backdrops with micro-fine metallic borders, establishing a multi-layered, bespoke dining room ambiance.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intimate, nocturnal dining experience. Contrast is achieved through warm luminescence rather than harsh polar values.

### Roles & Semantic Values

- **Canvas & Surface Base (`#121110`):** The foundational substrate; deep warm charcoal with umber undertones.
- **Surface Raised / Card Background (`#1C1A17`):** Elevated surfaces, tasting course containers, and interactive sheet layers.
- **Surface Overlay (`#22201D`):** Floating dialogs, menus, and micro-cards with 85–92% opacity backdrop filters.
- **Primary / Aged Gold Foil (`#D4AF37`):** Editorial highlights, active indicators, Michelin/award honors, and luxury badges.
- **Tertiary / Brilliant Gold (`#E5C158`):** Hover states, focused keylines, and luminous typographic touches.
- **Secondary / Velvety Cream (`#FBF9F5`):** Primary display headers, dish titles, and highest-priority body copy.
- **Muted Cream (`#C8C4BC`):** Ingredient descriptions, secondary body passages, and tasting notes.
- **Subtle Metallic Hairline (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`): Used for 1px structural borders and card dividers to evoke gold leaf leafing.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial cadence between literary grandeur and functional Swiss clarity:
- **Headings & Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Rhythmic high-contrast serifs with sculpted ball terminals. Headings should be styled in sentence case or title case; never forced uppercase for long titles.
- **Culinary Body & Annotations (`Manrope`):** Clean, geometric-humanist grotesque set with light-to-regular weights and generous line-height to deliver airiness and effortless readability against dark backgrounds.
- **Labels, Courses & Micro-Text:** Set exclusively in tracking-expanded uppercase `Manrope` (`0.14em` to `0.18em`), imbuing table service cards, harvest dates, and vintage years with timeless bespoke character.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column fluid grid, framed by quiet, generous perimeters:

- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12 columns, 48px margins, 32px gutters. Hero sections and degustation galleries honor generous vertical breathing space (`space-3xl`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px margins, 24px gutters. Secondary side-by-side tasting notes collapse into asymmetric full-width pairings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px margins, 16px gutters. Structural multi-column dish lineups reflow to vertical sensory cards with prominent imagery.
- **Micro-alignment:** UI details (ingredients, provenance badges, pricing) conform to strict vertical keylines to mirror the precision of culinary tweezers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through luminous tonal planes and subtle frosted optical effects rather than heavy drop shadows:

1. **Base Ground (Level 0):** Pure `#121110`, absorbing ambient light.
2. **Plated Surface (Level 1):** Solid `#1C1A17` combined with a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`). Zero blur shadow.
3. **Glass Tasting Tier (Level 2):** Semi-transparent `#22201D` with 90% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, accompanied by an inner top edge highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.08)`) and a subtle downward gold-tinted glow (`0 12px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`).
4. **Modal / Caviar Drawer (Level 3):** Frosted `#1A1916` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, defined by a continuous foil border (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`) and a deep atmospheric shadow (`0 24px 64px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`).

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette relies on **Level 1 (Soft)** roundedness. Elements utilize refined 4px to 8px corner radii, maintaining architectural poise and avoiding playful, overly rounded aesthetics:

- **Cards, Modals & Course Plates:** 4px radius (`0.25rem`). Clean, tailored, and razor-sharp like Japanese cutlery.
- **Interactive Badges & Gastronomy Tags:** 2px radius to strictly retain an artisan, pressed-paper label feel.
- **Circular Accents:** Applied exclusively to chef monograph portraits, vintage wax seals, and audio/video controls.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTA
- **Primary Gold Button:** Rich satin fill using `#D4AF37`, dark charcoal text (`#121110`), `font-weight: 600`, letter-spaced `0.08em` uppercase. Subtle inset bevel reflection. On hover, background shifts to `#E5C158` with an ethereal gold bloom (`0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Foil Outline Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` border, cream typography (`#FBF9F5`). On hover, the border becomes solid `#D4AF37` and the background transitions to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Text Link CTA:** Italicized serif or uppercase label followed by a fine brass arrow (`→`) that extends smoothly `+4px` on hover.

### Cards & Menu Folios
- **Dish Feature Card:** Dark charcoal background (`#1C1A17`) enveloped by a 1px border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`). Food photography dominates with a subtle 1.03x zoom on card hover. Tasting notes sit atop a frosted lower surface with a gradient feather.
- **Course Pairing Card:** Features an ultra-thin gold divider line separating dish description from vintage wine pairings and harvest origins.

### Badges, Accents & Tags
- **Culinary Badges:** Compact labels (e.g., "3 MICHELIN STARS", "AUTUMN EQUINOX") styled in `label-badge` typography, with a charcoal ground, micro-fine gold border, and gold text (`#D4AF37`).
- **Dietary & Provenance Tags:** Subdued, monochromatic cream outline (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.2)`) with cream text (`#C8C4BC`) that brightens upon hover.

### Inputs & Form Fields (Private Dining Inquiries)
- Clean, minimal baseline inputs. Dark background (`#1C1A17`) with a crisp bottom rule (`1px solid rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.2)`).
- Focused state transforms the border to `#D4AF37` accompanied by an elevated floating gold label in `label-caps`.
- Custom date and party selector pills styled in muted glass with a selected state outlined in brushed bronze.

### Lists & Degustation Indexes
- Numbered courses displayed in classical Roman or tabular figures with a warm gold treatment.
- Dashed leader lines or micro-spaced dots linking courses to culinary philosophy notes, preserving timeless grand-menu editorial formatting.

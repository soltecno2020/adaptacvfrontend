## Brand & Style

The brand evokes quiet luxury, discreet security, and bespoke European domestic care. It balances the warmth of nurturing childcare with the exacting, tailored precision of private household management. Drawing inspiration from Scandinavian domestic serenity (Kinfolk, Oliver Furniture) and Parisian couture elegance (Céline, The Row, Aesop), the aesthetic communicates effortless composure, discretion, and calm assurance.

The design philosophy adopts an elevated **Warm Minimalist Glass & Linen** aesthetic:
- **Atmospheric Warmth**: Soft cream backdrops substitute stark tech-white to evoke tactile linen, cashmere, and limestone.
- **Editorial Poise**: High-contrast, literary serif typography sits harmoniously beside immaculate, functional geometric sans.
- **Tactile Softness**: Translucent frosted surfaces, gentle pill contours, and whisper-delicate sand accents replace heavy structural borders or industrial shadows.
- **Bilingual European Polish**: Space is tuned specifically for international luxury clientele, effortlessly balancing French phrasing, English restraint, and cross-border domestic poise.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from natural domestic textures: French limestone, raw unbleached wool, dry Mediterranean sage, and aged Provençal terracotta.

- **Primary (`#24211E`) — Espresso Charcoal**: Deep, warm soot replacing harsh synthetic blacks. Used for primary editorial headings, core buttons, and high-legibility iconography.
- **Secondary (`#768271`) — Muted Sage**: An earthy, botanical herb tone conveying balance, wellness, and mindful childcare. Used for trust badges, verified background accents, and confirmed states.
- **Tertiary (`#B87A63`) — Soft Terracotta**: A warm, sun-dried clay tone signaling hospitality, culinary nourishment, and subtle calls-to-action without digital alarm.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0`) — Crème & Oatmeal**: The tactile foundation. Surfaces cascade from unbleached linen canvas (`#F9F6F0`) down to pale alabaster cards (`#FFFFFF`) and warm oat-milk dividers (`#EDE6DD`).
- **Accent Border — Fine Gold Sand (`#DFD5C6`)**: A metallic-tinted stone hue used for hairline borders and deliberate micro-separators.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts the haute-couture editorial presence of **Playfair Display** with the clean, legible ergonomics of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines & Narrative Moments**: Always rendered in Playfair Display. It establishes the rhythm of an archival lifestyle monograph. Italics are reserved for emotional concierge highlights (e.g., *“Gouvernante Privée”* or *“Bespoke Household Care”*).
- **Body & Service Specification**: Executed in Plus Jakarta Sans with generous line heights to ensure legible, fatigue-free reading during client onboarding and candidate reviews.
- **Bilingual Captions**: Small uppercase metadata (`label-caps`) uses wide tracking (`0.08em`) to accommodate both English and French terminology cleanly across compact viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid column structure engineered for handheld elegance, scaling seamlessly into tablet and editorial desktop showcases:

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 1.25rem outer margins and 1rem gutters. Touch targets adhere strictly to 48px minimum heights. Full-width frosted sheets ground the lower viewport.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 2.5rem margins and 1.5rem gutters. Dual-pane presentation balances candidate dossier summaries alongside booking schedules.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of 1280px to preserve photographic intimacy and typographic line length. Margins expand to 4rem.

Content relies on rhythmic breathing space: section breaks demand generous vertical gutters (`space-xl`), allowing high-res photography of serene interiors and vetted governesses to anchor the visual narrative.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects sharp artificial drop shadows in favor of ambient multi-layered diffusion, warm light refraction, and frosted material layers.

- **Base Canvas**: Solid warm crème (`#F9F6F0`).
- **Frosted Glass Layer (Floating Concierge Cards)**: 
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`.
  - Backdrop Blur: `16px` with saturating filter at `120%`.
  - Border: 1px continuous solid `rgba(223, 213, 198, 0.65)` (Fine Sand).
  - Ambient Shadow: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(36, 33, 30, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(36, 33, 30, 0.02)`.
- **Raised Interactive Layer (Drawers, Modals, Action Sheets)**:
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`.
  - Backdrop Blur: `24px`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(223, 213, 198, 0.9)`.
  - Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(36, 33, 30, 0.08), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(36, 33, 30, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature combines organic softness with structured classicism:

- **Containers & Glass Cards**: Employ `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) on mobile viewports, transitioning to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for interior nested segments.
- **Interactive Badges & Meta Pills**: Fully radiused pills (`9999px`) provide contrast against rectangular cards.
- **Form Controls & Search Bars**: Calibrated at 0.75rem (12px) curvature to mirror bespoke physical stationery.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Concierge Action**: Solid Espresso Charcoal (`#24211E`) fill with Crème (`#F9F6F0`) text. Roundedness: pill (`9999px`). Height: 52px. Micro-interaction: scales to `0.98` with subtle opacity reduction (`0.92`) on press.
- **Secondary / Inquiry**: Frosted pill with 1px Sand Border (`#DFD5C6`). Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`. Text: `#24211E`. Hover: Background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` with warm shadow.
- **Tertiary Accent**: Delicate Terracotta (`#B87A63`) text with understated 1px underline that expands smoothly on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges**: Low-saturation tonal pills (e.g., Muted Sage at 12% opacity with solid `#768271` text for “Montessori Certified” or “Norland Trained”). 
- Padding: 6px vertical, 14px horizontal. Border: 1px hairline matching the fill's tint at 20% opacity.

### Concierge & Personnel Cards
- Built as frosted translucent slabs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- Structure: Includes caregiver/housekeeper portrait (soft-edge 12px radius, warm natural desaturation), multilingual accreditation tags, years of discrete placement experience, and an understated “Request Dossier” action.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`. 
- Border: 1px sand tone (`#DFD5C6`). 
- Focus state: Border transitions to Espresso Charcoal (`#24211E`) with zero glowing ring; instead, a soft inner ring `inset 0 0 0 1px #24211E` reinforces the sharp, tailored look.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom circular indicators. Selected state fills with Espresso Charcoal (`#24211E`) containing a delicate Sand check icon. Unselected displays a subtle `#DFD5C6` perimeter over a translucent white disc.

### Service Tier Selector (Bespoke Housekeeping & Childcare)
- Segmented pill container (`#EDE6DD`) with a gliding white frosted thumb that floats beneath the active selection, supported by soft tactile micro-vibration cues on touch devices.
